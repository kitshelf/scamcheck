# Romance marker pack (alpha)

**Verified as of: 2026-11-20.**
**Status: alpha research pack.** Markers sourced and calibration-lined; fixtures pending. The romance family ships last (v0.3.0) because its register is the most delicate — the kit names patterns, never diagnoses a person's feelings. A screen that loads this pack must say so.
**Sources.** CFTC customer advisories on relationship-investment fraud (2024–2025), including "Dating or Defrauding" and the six warning signs of online financial romance fraud; AARP's pig-butchering and wrong-number-text explainers; state regulator warnings (WA, WI DFI, 2025). **Corpus note (2026-11-20):** the corpus's `wrong`-number dialogue type sits on the *safe* side of `scam-dialogues/` (200 rows, labelled not-scam), so the corpus does **not** evidence RO-PR1 — the marker keeps its CFTC/AARP sourcing. RO-I1's secrecy shape *is* supported: `keep this … confidential` 265/1, `between us` 80/5 in the CLAIR advance-fee letters. Recorded in [`../../corpus/TUNING.md`](../../corpus/TUNING.md).

**How to use.** Fire a marker only on the message's own quoted words, and only when its *Not-a-marker-when* condition does not hold. Same labels as the spine. The tone rule doubles here: calm, no mockery, no "you're being scammed" addressed at the person — the markers speak.

## Markers

**RO-PR1 · The wrong-number opener.** A stranger's mistaken text that keeps talking.
Tells: "Sorry, I think I sent this to the wrong number… but since we're talking—" (the documented pig-butchering opener across CFTC, AARP, and state warnings).
Not a marker when: a genuine wrong number ends when corrected — one apology, no continuation, no profile, no investment talk. The continuation is the marker, not the mistake.

**RO-PR2 · Never met, video always broken.** Months of contact, zero face-to-face.
Tells: a camera that fails every scheduled call · refusal to meet.
Not a marker when: a video call happens and a meeting is planned. Distance is ordinary; permanent avoidance is the pattern.

**RO-A1 · The rich, attractive stranger.** Success woven into the introduction.
Tells: photos too polished · early mentions of wealth, trading wins, or "crypto luck."
Not a marker when: an ordinary person talks about ordinary work. Polished-plus-profitable is the costume the CFTC advisories describe (fake profiles, images, videos, and voices).

**RO-P1 · "Let me teach you to invest."** Affection becomes a lesson about money.
Tells: "I'll show you how I do it" · a little-known platform or app to deposit into.
Not a marker when: never. Investment advice from someone you have never met is the center of the pattern — every regulator source says the same sentence: never invest through someone you met online.

**RO-P2 · Pay to withdraw.** Fees to unlock "earnings."
Tells: "pay the tax/verification fee and the platform releases your profits."
Not a marker when: never. Legitimate platforms never charge to give you your own money back; the same mechanics as the FTC's task scams, pointed at a relationship.

**RO-P3 · The crisis after affection.** The ask arrives wearing an emergency.
Tells: a hospital bill, a customs fee, a plane ticket — once trust is built.
Not a marker when: real life has real emergencies; the marker is the *sequence* — a stranger, a fast bond, then money.

**RO-I1 · "Don't tell anyone."** Secrecy as intimacy.
Tells: "please keep this between us — your friends wouldn't understand" · "keep this confidential."
Not a marker when: private is not the same as forbidden. The bare words "don't tell" do not fire — the corpus's safe side uses them as often; the marker is a request to hide the relationship from everyone who could judge it, a documented warning sign (CFTC). Corpus-attested: `keep this … confidential` 265/1, `between us` 80/5 in the CLAIR advance-fee letters.

**RO-U1 · Rushed intimacy.** Love arrives on a schedule.
Tells: "I've never felt this way so fast" · declarations within days.
Not a marker when: affection is ordinary and the pace is mutual. Rushing the bond is the fattening stage of the documented pattern; the ask always comes later.

## The romance verify checklist

1. **Reverse-image the photos.** Stolen photo sets are the norm in the documented pattern; the search shows the original.
2. **Propose a video call.** Watch the new excuse. Then propose another.
3. **Tell one real person.** A friend outside the thread reads it in minutes; the pattern survives isolation and dies in daylight — the secrecy marker (RO-I1) exists because the ask cannot survive a witness.
4. **Never move money** — no "small first investment," no fee, no platform someone met online introduced. [CONFIRM: what, if anything, has been sent — the recovery branch applies without judgment.]

## The recovery pointer

If money was sent — the documented pattern's losses run catastrophic — the spine's recovery branch applies without blame: report to `reportfraud.ftc.gov` and `ic3.gov`, and the FBI's IC3 recovery-team process exists partly for this family. No odds. No shame. The pattern is industrial, and the person it caught is not the fool — the pattern is.
