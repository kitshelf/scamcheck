# Synthetic Multi-Turn Scam and Non-Scam Phone Conversation Dataset with Agentic Personalities

## Dataset Description
The Synthetic Multi-Turn Scam and Non-Scam Phone Dialogue Dataset with Agentic Personalities is an enhanced collection of simulated phone conversations between two AI agents, one acting as a scammer or non-scammer and the other as an innocent receiver. Each dialogue is labeled as either a scam or non-scam interaction. This dataset is designed to help develop and evaluate models for detecting and classifying various types of phone-based scams, incorporating diverse receiver personalities to better mimic real-world scenarios.

## Dataset Structure
The dataset consists of four columns:

- **dialogue**: The transcribed conversation between the caller and receiver.
- **personality**: The personality type of the innocent receiver in the conversation.
- **type**: The specific type of scam or non-scam interaction.
- **label**: A binary label indicating whether the conversation is a scam (1) or not (0).


In the dialogue, the caller is annotated as **Suspect** while the receiver is annotated as **Innocent**.

## Scam Types (label 1)
- **ssn**: Social security number scams, where the scammer attempts to obtain the victim's SSN.
- **refund**: Refund scams, where the scammer tries to convince the victim that they are owed a refund.
- **support**: Technical support scams, where the scammer impersonates a support representative to gain access to the victim's computer or personal information.
- **reward**: Reward scams, such as those involving gift cards, where the scammer promises a reward in exchange for personal information or money.

## Non-Scam Types (label 0)
- **delivery**: Legitimate delivery confirmation calls.
- **insurance**: Genuine insurance sales calls.
- **appointment**: Legitimate appointment scheduling or reminder calls.
- **wrong**: Wrong number calls for various reasons.

## Receiver Personalities
The innocent receiver is characterized by one of the following eight personalities, adding depth and variability to the interactions:
- **Aggressive**
- **Anxious**
- **Confused**
- **Distracted**
- **Greedy**
- **Polite**
- **Skeptical**

## Dataset Creation
The dialogues in this dataset were synthetically generated by allowing two AI agents to converse, with one designated as the scammer or non-scammer and the other embodying one of the eight innocent receiver personalities. This approach ensures a dynamic interaction that reflects the diverse ways individuals may respond to potential scams. The dataset was created using Autogen and Together Inference API, leveraging advanced AI capabilities to generate realistic and varied conversations between two AI agents.

## Intended Use
This dataset is intended for research and development in natural language processing, specifically for building models to detect and classify phone-based scams. By providing a labeled dataset of scam and non-scam conversations with varied receiver personalities, researchers can develop and evaluate algorithms to help protect individuals from falling victim to phone scams.

## Limitations and Ethical Considerations
As the dialogues in this dataset are synthetically generated, they may not capture all the nuances and variations found in real-world phone interactions. Additionally, while efforts have been made to create realistic conversations, there may be biases present in the generated dialogues.

Users of this dataset should be aware of the potential limitations and biases and should use the data responsibly. The dataset should not be used to make decisions that could harm individuals or groups.
